Homework 8a - Interactive Story (Part 1)

Story Description

The story takes place in a apocalyptic world, where humanity is falling, and need the help of androids to save themselves from extinction. The invading force is a group of evil hostile aliens, that plan to destroy humanity and steal its resources for its own personal gain. Humanity have created androids, elite robots that are tasked with fighting the aliens and retaking the world from them. The story will follow one Android named Valkyr-9 or Val. The story will focus on Val's point of view and how she will have to go through many choices that will determine the future of humanity. Some secrets and tricks will be made along the way.
