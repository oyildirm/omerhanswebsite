Eye Animation

This animated gif was created using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop. I first designed the eye in Illustrator, separating the eyelid into its own layer. Then I exported the file as a PSD and used Photoshop’s timeline to create the blinking animation by duplicating and adjusting the eyelid layer across multiple frames. The final result creates a smooth blinking motion that adds life to the original illustration.
