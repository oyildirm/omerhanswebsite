Creative Statement

My work is focused on the weirdness of the world, the unusual but fascinating aspects of life that create mystery and ask the question of how. The inspiration for many of the works I produce comes from uncanny images online known as liminal spaces. The emptiness of a world can still feel familiar — the quiet yet loud parts of reality left in the dark. A brief image can mean nothing to one person but everything to another, and that is what I want to accomplish.
